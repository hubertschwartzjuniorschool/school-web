About Us

Nurturing young minds with purpose & care

At Hubert Schwaz Junior School, we believe every child carries a spark of brilliance. Our mission is to ignite that spark through a rigorous yet joyful academic environment that values curiosity, kindness, and creativity in equal measure.

Guided by experienced educators and grounded in time-honored values, we shape well-rounded learners equipped to thrive academically, socially, and emotionally — today and well into the future.

Admissions

Basic Requirements

Please have the following documents ready before submitting your application.

Admission Application Form

Fully completed and signed by parent or guardian.

2 Passport-Sized Photos

Recent color photographs of the child with a plain background.

Previous Report Cards

Academic transcripts from the previous school attended.

Birth Certificate or Health Card

A clear copy to confirm the child's identity and age.

Proof of Payment

Receipt showing the application fee has been paid.
